This devotional is created to offer spiritual encouragement and point hearts back to God.
All Scripture quotations are taken from the Holy Bible, New Living Translation (NLT).

---

I am so grateful you are here. This devotional is a 24-day journey to help you draw close to Jesus. As you read these love letters, you will be reminded of God's faithfulness, receive comfort for your heart, break free in your mind, find healing in love and relationships, and step into the beauty of becoming new.

Let each day be a quiet moment with Jesus. Let the words settle in. Let the verses stay with you. If tears come, let them.

I pray that as you turn these pages, you feel more seen, more loved, and more aware of His presence. May you remember you are not alone, and you never have been.

May you rest in this: You are forever His, and He is forever yours.

Rose Renuu

---

SECTION 1: GOD'S FAITHFULNESS

---

Day 1: Forever With You

My Child,

I am with you in every moment and every chapter of your life. When happiness fills your heart, I am your God of blessings. When you are broken by pain, I am your God of healing. When you are wronged, I am your God of justice. When you are overwhelmed by fear, I am your God of courage. When guilt weighs heavily on you, I am your God of forgiveness. When shame makes you feel unworthy, I am your God of grace. When you see no way forward, I am your God of miracles.

I am the same yesterday, today, and forever. You will never walk through a struggle or a joyful moment without Me by your side. In every season, I am your God.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

I am with you always, even to the end of the age.
Matthew 28:20 NLT

---

Day 2: Gentle Knock

My Child,

I have never stopped pursuing you. Even when you walked away. Even when you did not know Me. Even when you thought you were doing it all on your own strength. I was still there, guarding you and guiding you. I was whispering your name in the quiet moments when you did not yet recognize it was Me.

I placed the right people in your life. I closed doors that were not meant for you and opened ones no one else could. I held you up when the weight of it all should have crushed you. I performed miracles, so one day you would look back and realize it was Me.

I understand your thoughts and concerns can grow so loud that they drown out My gentle voice. Yet I am always near, gently knocking on the door of your heart, waiting for you to let Me in. I am not asking for perfection, only the real you. When you are ready, I will sit with you, speak with you, and stay with you.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

"Look! I stand at the door and knock. If you hear my voice and open the door, I will come in, and we will share a meal together as friends."
Revelation 3:20 NLT

---

Day 3: Steady Guide

My Child,

I am near. I am faithful. Do not be afraid. I know that life can feel like walking through a maze. You may not see what lies ahead; you only see what lies in front of you. It may feel uncertain, filled with twists and turns.

Remember this: I see the entire maze from above. My presence will always be with you and direct you on the right path. Even in your confusion, I will gently guide your steps. Your ears will hear My direction. I will tell you which way you should go, whether to the right or to the left. My word is a lamp that keeps you steady and a light that keeps you from getting lost.

Seek My will in all you do and trust Me where I lead you. Be strong and courageous. I started your story, and I promise to finish it more beautifully than you can imagine.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

The Lord says, "I will guide you along the best pathway for your life. I will advise you and watch over you."
Psalm 32:8 NLT

---

Day 4: Promise Keeper

My Child,

I cherish you with the purest form of love. A love that is unchanging and unconditional. Rest in the truth that when I make a promise, I always keep it. You can trust every promise I have made to you. I do not lie, and I will not fail you.

Think back on the times when everything seemed uncertain, and you could not see the way forward. Yet somehow, the right door opened, the help arrived, and a way was made. That was Me, keeping My promise to be your protector and provider.

My plans for you are good. I will fulfill them, even when you struggle and doubt. I will remove the mountain in the way of My Will for your life. Hold tightly to My promises, say them aloud, and let them strengthen your faith. Keep walking with Me. I will carry you through it all.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

God is not a man, so he does not lie. He is not human, so he does not change his mind. Has he ever spoken and failed to act? Has he ever promised and not carried it through?
Numbers 23:19 NLT

---

SECTION 2: HEALING OF THE HEART

---

Day 5: Heaven Holds You Close

My Child,

I am close to you, and I feel your broken heart. I understand losing someone you love is a heaviness that is not easy to bear. Every day you miss their presence and love. Their voices you can hear deep in your heart. The memories that fade into the distance, yet never truly disappear.

Your pain is deep, and it is real. Remember that I am the God of life; your loved ones are not lost, they are with Me. Through My Son's resurrection, death is not the end, but a new beginning. It is a reminder that this world is not your home; your citizenship is in Heaven. The separation you experience is only temporary.

Hold tightly to this hope. There will come a day when there will be no more death and no more tears. You will reunite with your loved ones. This time, you will never have to say goodbye.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

We want you to know what will happen to the believers who have died so you will not grieve like people who have no hope. For since we believe that Jesus died and was raised to life again, we also believe that when Jesus returns, God will bring back with him the believers who have died.
1 Thessalonians 4:13-14 NLT

---

Day 6: Release And Receive

My Child,

I understand it's not easy to see people come and go in your life. The sorrow of losing someone you thought was meant to stay or bear the pain of rejection. Trust that every bond and connection is a piece of the larger story I am writing for you.

I see and hear things you don't. I know who is meant to walk with you and who is not. I know who will lift you higher and who will only bring you down, distract you, or lead you away from the path I have set for you. You don't need to win anyone's approval or convince anyone to stay in your life. The right people will be there without you needing to strive or perform.

Some people were meant for a season. It's okay to grieve the goodbyes. Allow Me to heal your wounds and take care of the burdens you have been carrying. Release what hurts you, so it does not harm what I am giving you. Let go of what's behind and trust Me with what's ahead.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

For everything there is a season, a time for every activity under heaven.
Ecclesiastes 3:1 NLT

---

Day 7: Hold On

My Child,

I feel your pain and the uncertainty in your heart. I know that as you go through life, there may be seasons of suffering, heartbreak, loss, delays, or setbacks. I understand these things don't make sense, and you find yourself asking, "Why am I going through this?"

Please know that I am right here with you. I am working on your story in ways you might not recognize right now, but one day, you will see how every piece fits together into something beautiful. Every trial and tribulation is part of a greater plan that is leading you to a place of deep understanding and peace.

Do not be troubled. I got you and your life under control. Trust in My hands that hold all knowledge. Keep your eyes on Me in the storm because brighter days are ahead.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

Jesus replied, "You don't understand now what I am doing, but someday you will."
John 13:7 NLT

---

Day 8: Nothing Is Wasted

My Child,

I cherish your obedience. I delight in all the good things you do in My name. I see it all, even when you feel like no one else does. I see you offer kindness when it's not returned. I see you choose to forgive when it's hard. I see you give generously when you don't have enough. I see you show up in faith when it's easier to give up.

Never forget that I understand your pain, and I bottle every tear you have shed. In moments when you question if it's all worth it, know that nothing you do for Me is ever wasted. Heaven records everything you do, no matter how small it may seem on earth.

I am proud of you. I am working in ways you cannot see. What you plant in love, I will multiply into a harvest of good.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

Always work enthusiastically for the Lord, for you know that nothing you do for the Lord is ever useless.
1 Corinthians 15:58 NLT

---

Day 9: Beauty Within

My Child,

What I see in your heart matters far more than what the world sees on the outside. I treasure a pure heart, for it is the place where I dwell, speak, and move in your life. This is why your priceless heart is the wellspring of all you do, determining your thoughts, desires, and actions. I ask you to guard it carefully in this world.

Strive for purity no matter what you face. You may go through difficulties and challenges. People may hurt you, deceive you, or let you down. Yet do not let these circumstances cause bitterness to take root and harden your heart. Do not allow anyone or anything change you, nor dim your light.

When your heart is wounded, come to Me. I will restore what was lost, mend what is broken, and fill you with My peace. With a pure heart, you will walk in peace, freedom, and wholeness, and through you, others will see Me.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

People judge by outward appearance, but the Lord looks at the heart.
1 Samuel 16:7 NLT

---

Day 10: Godly Self Love

My Child,

I see how deeply you feel others' pain as if it were your own. The way your compassionate heart sees the wounds and fears others hide. However, in lifting their burdens, you have neglected your own. You were never meant to lose yourself in the process of loving others.

I see the heaviness in your heart. The unspoken feelings you have pushed aside. The moments you have questioned whether your needs even matter. I want to remind you that they do. You matter.

If your love requires you to abandon yourself, it is not the kind of love I commanded. I said to love your neighbor as yourself, not instead of yourself. I name you worthy, I speak life over you, and I protect you with boundaries. Let that be the standard by which you treat yourself, and it will become the standard by which you love others.

As you learn to care for your own heart, you also learn to care for someone else's. Love yourself as I love you.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

Love your neighbor as yourself.
Mark 12:31 NLT

---

SECTION 3: SET FREE

---

Day 11: Rest Is Holy

My Child,

I see how your mind keeps running even when your body sits still. I see the quiet guilt that whispers, "You should be doing more." I see how your heart stays on alert, as if slowing down means you will fall behind or fall apart.

I want you to know that rest is not a weakness; it is restoration. From the very beginning, I built rest into life itself. On the seventh day, I rested. It is to show you a holy rhythm. Yes, your work, purpose, and obedience matter. Yet so does stopping, breathing, and delighting in what I have already done.

When you refuse to pause, you start to believe your value is tied to your output. Your heart starts to confuse striving with faithfulness. I never asked you to live that way. I created you to walk with Me, not to race without Me.

Remember that I hold all things together. Come to Me. Let rest be your worship, let stillness be your trust, and let peace be your home again.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

On the seventh day God had finished his work of creation, so he rested from all his work.
Genesis 2:2 NLT

---

Day 12: The Peace You Need

My Child,

Only you and I know the real pain engraved deep within you. You have suppressed it, yet it never fully goes away. Sometimes you try to ignore it, and most of the time, you swallow it with distractions. While escaping reality, you escape your pain. You get that sense of freedom, the boost of confidence, the instant happiness, or the numbness that shuts down every ache. You chase after more of this peace, trying to fill every void. Still, it lasts only for so long. Now you find yourself trying to escape the very thing that was supposed to bring you peace.

Do not lose hope, My love. I see you, understand you, and I will give you the peace you have been longing for. My peace is real and lasting. I do not give as the world gives. It is not based on any circumstance or distractions. I will carry you through your pain and heal every brokenness within you.

Give Me a chance and call on My name. Bring Me what you've been carrying, and I will trade it for My peace.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

"I am leaving you with a gift- peace of mind and heart. And the peace I give is a gift the world cannot give. So don't be troubled or afraid."
John 14:27 NLT

---

Day 13: Quiet The Noise

My Child,

How you think holds a lot of power. This is why the enemy will often attack you through your thoughts. He knows that if he can plant seeds of doubt and fear, they will take root, shape your emotions, and ultimately your actions.

When you think you are not capable, you will feel discouraged and want to give up. When you think anxious thoughts, you will feel fearful, hesitant, and paralyzed. But when you think about My faithfulness, you will feel peace and walk in confidence.

You don't need to listen to the enemy's lies. Hold every thought captive and make it obedient to Me. When you have thoughts that weigh you down, replace them with My truth. I understand how easy it is to feel lost in them, but remember that I am the still, small voice, speaking peace into your heart.

Fix your mind on Me and turn down the noise that is stealing your joy. Rest in My promises, knowing nothing can come against you, because I am always by your side. Put on My full armor and guard your mind with My truth.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

You will keep in perfect peace all who trust in you, all whose thoughts are fixed on you.
Isaiah 26:3 NLT

---

Day 14: The False Echoes

My Child,

I am with you in every moment of doubt. Every battle between your thoughts, the enemy's lies, and My truth. I speak to you with peace and not panic, with love and not shame.

When the voices inside you grow loud, remember this. I am not the voice that condemns or belittles you. I don't speak to you in words that tear you down or use your past against you. That is the enemy who seeks to steal your peace and convince you that you are not worthy.

When the enemy tells you that you are not enough, remember that I made you wonderful. When you feel alone, know that I am right here with you. When your thoughts turn to worry, call on Me. I am your strength and safe place.

You don't need to be perfect to hear Me. Come as you are. I will quiet the false echoes, and My peace will settle your heart.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

My sheep listen to my voice; I know them, and they follow me.
John 10:27 NLT

---

Day 15: Inner Confidence

My Child,

The world may try to convince you that your worth is measured by what you achieve or how you appear. But none of those things define who you truly are. Your value does not come from the approval of others nor from the things you possess. It comes from Me. You are already valuable because I have called you Mine.

People may look at the outward appearance, but I look at your heart. You don't need to chase validation because your worth is secure in Me, not in their voices. When your confidence is rooted in Me, nothing can take it away from you. The world cannot define you because I already have.

Do not build your identity on the earthly treasures that moths and rust destroy, and thieves can steal. Instead, store your treasures in heaven that endures forever. This is where you will find true fulfillment. Follow Me, and you will not miss what I have prepared for you.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

This is what the Lord says: "Don't let the wise boast in their wisdom, or the powerful boast in their power, or the rich boast in their riches. But those who wish to boast should boast in this alone: that they truly know me and understand that I am the Lord who demonstrates unfailing love and who brings justice and righteousness to the earth and that I delight in these things. I, the Lord, have spoken!
Jeremiah 9:23-24 NLT

---

Day 16: Always Listening

My Child,

I am the God of miracles. Nothing is impossible for Me. You can trust in who I am, My power, and My promises to you. I know there are times when you feel like I am distant or that your prayers go unheard. Yet, I promise you that when you pray, I am fully attentive. Just because I did not give you what you asked for does not mean I did not hear you.

I care about you deeply. I would never want to give you anything that would lead you away from Me or My plans for your life. I can move any mountain in your way. If I don't remove it, it is because I am still guiding you toward the heart of My will. No matter how I lead you, you are safe under My wings.

Keep your faith strong. I am actively working.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

Trust in the Lord with all your heart; do not depend on your own understanding. Seek his will in all you do, and he will show you which path to take.
Proverbs 3:5-6 NLT

---

SECTION 4: LOVE AND RELATIONSHIPS

---

Day 17: Family And Friends

My Child,

You are blessed with special people in your life. I know the love you have for them. I see how much you want to protect and provide. You want the best; to see them happy, healed, and fulfilled. I understand that desire because I feel the same about you.

Remember, just as you belong to Me, your loved ones belong to Me as well. You are all My beloved children. My love for them is just as profound. I want you to let go of the weight you carry, trying to hold everything together. Your relationships are not meant to burden or overwhelm you with fear.

Trust Me with your loved ones as you trust Me with yourself. Pray for them and place them in My hands. I promise to take great care of them, just as I take care of you. I will carry you both through everything that this life brings.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

The Lord says, "I will rescue those who love me. I will protect those who trust in my name. When they call on me, I will answer; I will be with them in trouble. I will rescue and honor them. I will reward them with a long life and give them my salvation."
Psalm 91:14-16 NLT

---

Day 18: Loving Again

My Child,

You were once someone who was not afraid. Not afraid to love deeply, to go the extra mile for others, and to laugh with your whole heart. Now, you have locked your heart away. You fear that if you let your guard down, you will be disappointed again. I know it feels hard to trust after being let down so many times. I understand that you are afraid to love again.

Yet, My child, you were not created to live numb to love. I don't want your past wounds to stop you from experiencing the beauty and joys of love. I know you were only trying to survive what wounded you.

I am not asking you to love blindly. I am guiding you to love wisely. Come to Me and let Me show you what safe love feels like. Let Me trade your walls for healthy boundaries, and your fear for discernment. Loving again does not make you weak. Rather, it makes you more like Me.

Take one small step towards softness again. I will not let you walk alone. Let Me hold your heart before you give it away again. Come to Me first, and I will heal what hurt you. I will lead you into love that is patient, kind, and secure.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

Love is patient and kind. Love is not jealous or boastful or proud or rude. It does not demand its own way. It is not irritable, and it keeps no record of being wronged. It does not rejoice about injustice but rejoices whenever the truth wins out. Love never gives up, never loses faith, is always hopeful, and endures through every circumstance.
1 Corinthians 13:4-7 NLT

---

Day 19: Letting Go

My Child,

You live in a broken world, and I see every injustice. I understand how deeply it hurts when others harm you and the ones you love. I see the anger you carry. The desire to get even. The need to make sure those who hurt you truly understand your pain.

Yet I have not called you to live that way. Paying back may give you temporary satisfaction, but it plants the roots of bitterness in your heart. Revenge does not punish them. It steals your peace. It makes you live out of the wound.

I don't want you to carry these burdens that were never yours to carry. Trust that I am the righteous Judge, and My justice never fails. I will make all things right in My perfect timing. I ask you to choose forgiveness and release your pain to Me. I will cover you with My peace, restore what was lost, and give rest for your soul.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

Don't let evil conquer you, but conquer evil by doing good.
Romans 12:21 NLT

---

Day 20: Forgiveness

My Child,

There is so much joy waiting for you on the other side of forgiveness. I know what they did hurt you deeply. I am not asking you to forget what happened. I am asking you to let Me heal what it left behind. Forgiveness is not saying it was okay. Forgiveness is releasing what happened into My hands, so it no longer rules your heart.

You have been carrying this pain so tightly by replaying it, reliving it, and bracing for it. I see how heavy it has been. I don't want you to stay trapped in what wounded you.

Bring Me the truth, every detail, every feeling, every tear. I can handle your honesty. Whisper, "Jesus, help me forgive." Then give it to Me again tomorrow and the next day until your heart feels lighter. Every step toward forgiveness is a step toward peace.

I will heal you as you let go. I will not rush you, and I will not leave you. I will take the burden from your hands. Reflect on how much grace I have poured over your life. Let the mercy you have received become the mercy you release.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

Make allowance for each other's faults, and forgive anyone who offends you. Remember, the Lord forgave you, so you must forgive others.
Colossians 3:13 NLT

---

SECTION 5: MADE NEW

---

Day 21: Turn To Me

My Child,

I am a loving, kind, and merciful God. No matter how far you have strayed away, I am always here waiting for you to come back home. How you feel does not change how I feel about you.

I see you when you are empty. When shame makes you hide. When you feel far and don't know how to return. When you are weighed down by everything you are carrying. Even when you can't feel My presence through your pain, I am here. I will never abandon you in your struggles. When it's hardest to turn to Me, that is when I am closest to you.

You don't have to have all the answers or even the right words. I ask that you keep coming to Me, confess your wrongs, and I will continue to work in you. I am faithful to forgive. Reach for Me, and I will meet you right where you are. I will wrap you in My comfort and guide you one step at a time.

My heart is not only for your rescue, but for your full restoration.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

In the same way, there is more joy in heaven over one lost sinner who repents and returns to God than over ninety-nine others who are righteous and haven't strayed away!
Luke 15:7 NLT

---

Day 22: Victory Over Temptation

My Child,

I understand your struggles and the battles you face. You may feel trapped by the temptations of this world. That inner conflict of knowing what is right but feeling pulled the other way. The enemy might whisper lies, making you believe you have already lost the fight, and giving in is the only choice.

Yet My power is stronger than anything that comes against you. You were never meant to do this alone. My Son Jesus relied on Me for strength, and not His own willpower. The same is true for you. You need My Spirit working within you to overcome your weaknesses.

The more you start to focus on Me and use My word to fight the desires, the stronger you will become. In the same way, the enemy left Jesus, as you stand firm in Me, nothing will have power over you. My presence will never leave your side, and together we will break these chains.

You will no longer be controlled by temptation. You will live in freedom and peace.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

This High Priest of ours understands our weaknesses, for he faced all of the same testings we do, yet he did not sin. So let us come boldly to the throne of our gracious God. There we will receive his mercy, and we will find grace to help us when we need it most.
Hebrews 4:15-16 NLT

---

Day 23: Living Spirit

My Child,

I knew you before you were born. I formed you with purpose, and your identity is found in Me. The world often pressures you to fit in, shaping who you are and what you value. This noise can pull you toward approval, performance, and expectations that were never meant to define you.

Take a moment and reflect on where you have been following the world's voice instead of Mine. Bring it to Me. My truth will set you free and lead you into lasting joy.

My word is like a mirror that reveals what is true about you and the world around you. It reaches the deepest parts of you, bringing conviction that heals, correction that protects, and clarity that steadies you. Let My word soften your heart, renew your mind, and realign your perspective.

Allow Me to transform you from the inside out, until you begin to see yourself and your life through My eyes.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

For the word of God is alive and powerful. It is sharper than the sharpest two edged sword, cutting between soul and spirit, between joint and marrow. It exposes our innermost thoughts and desires.
Hebrews 4:12 NLT

---

Day 24: Paid By Love

My Child,

I came down to earth to show you the way. Not to watch from a distance, but to walk with you, to feel what you feel, and to carry what you could not carry. I gave My life not because you had to prove your worth, but because I love you. Even in your broken places, I saw beauty. In your wandering, I saw someone worth rescuing. I came for you without hesitation.

My love is stronger than the darkness you have faced. My mercy is more powerful than your past. You no longer need to strive to be enough or make sacrifices to reach Me. I became the sacrifice. I took your place so you can take My hand.

I defeated death and rose again, so you can rise into new life with Me. You rise above shame, above fear, above the grip of sin. You rise into purpose, into identity, and into the fullness of being My beloved child.

The tomb is empty, and your future is full. I did it for you.

Forever Yours,
Heavenly Father

Though he was God, he did not think of equality with God as something to cling to. Instead, he gave up his divine privileges; he took the humble position of a slave and was born as a human being. When he appeared in human form, he humbled himself in obedience to God and died a criminal's death on a cross.
Philippians 2:6-8 NLT

About the Author

Rose Renuu is a writer and content creator whose life was transformed when she encountered the love of Jesus in the middle of her darkest season. The words God placed on her heart during that time became the foundation of Forever Yours, a devotional written to help others hear His voice and feel His healing presence.

Rose creates heartfelt Love Notes online that remind people they are seen and loved, and that they are never walking alone. She now lives in Los Angeles, dedicating her life to spreading hope and healing through her writing and faith-filled content.
