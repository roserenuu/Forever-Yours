# Brand Reference Notes

## Aesthetic
Soft, minimal, faith-based motivational quotes with an elegant editorial feel.

## Color Palette
- Light blue: ~#D6E4F0
- Dark charcoal: ~#3A3A3A
- Blush pink: ~#E8CFC4
- Pale pink: ~#F5E4E0
- Warm beige: ~#E8E2D8

## Typography
- Serif fonts throughout (editorial/classic feel)
- Mix of bold, italic, and regular weights
- Occasional large drop caps
- Muted text colors that complement backgrounds

## Tone
- Encouraging, gentle, faith-driven
- Short phrases: "Keep going.", "This too shall pass.", "You are not forgotten.", "god is with you."
- Lowercase intentional in some cases for softness
