# Forever Yours - Brand Reference Guide

## Typography Styles & Matching Google Fonts

### 1. Editorial Serif (Primary Headlines)
**Use for:** "Keep going.", "HIS LOVE CHANGES EVERYTHING", "You are not forgotten." (bold)
- **Google Font:** Playfair Display
- **Weights:** Bold 700, Extra Bold 800, Black 900
- **Style:** High-contrast, elegant, commanding
- **CSS:** `font-family: 'Playfair Display', serif;`

### 2. Delicate Italic Serif (Soft Quotes)
**Use for:** "god is with you.", "one day at a time.", "You are not forgotten." (italic)
- **Google Font:** Cormorant Garamond
- **Weights:** Light 300 italic, Regular 400 italic
- **Style:** Thin, graceful, whisper-like
- **CSS:** `font-family: 'Cormorant Garamond', serif; font-style: italic;`

### 3. Flowing Script (Cursive Accents)
**Use for:** "She changed.", "you are deeply loved."
- **Google Font:** Dancing Script (versatile) or Great Vibes (formal)
- **Weights:** Regular 400, Bold 700
- **Style:** Handwritten elegance, personal touch
- **CSS:** `font-family: 'Dancing Script', cursive;`

### 4. Bold Display Serif with Character (Retro/Statement)
**Use for:** "a mindset shift that altered my brain chemistry forever:", "hello, December.", "You asked God for a sign and here it is."
- **Google Font:** Fraunces
- **Weights:** Bold 700, Black 900
- **Style:** Retro, editorial, slightly playful with swash italics
- **CSS:** `font-family: 'Fraunces', serif;`

### 5. Casual Handwriting (Personal/Raw)
**Use for:** "Dear God, I'm putting February in Your hands.", "Next time you want to feel rich..."
- **Google Font:** Caveat
- **Weights:** Regular 400, Medium 500
- **Style:** Authentic, messy-beautiful, personal
- **CSS:** `font-family: 'Caveat', cursive;`

### 6. Bold Condensed Sans (Impact Statements)
**Use for:** "Nobody talks about how much lighter life is...", "YOU ASKED GOD FOR A SIGN THIS FEBRUARY"
- **Google Font:** Oswald
- **Weights:** Semi Bold 600, Bold 700
- **Style:** Heavy, condensed, uppercase impact
- **CSS:** `font-family: 'Oswald', sans-serif; text-transform: uppercase;`

### 7. Clean Bold Serif (Warm Statements)
**Use for:** "Stop trying to figure everything out today.", "When IT'S HARDEST, GOD IS CLOSEST", "My Child, you look ahead..."
- **Google Font:** Lora
- **Weights:** Medium 500, Semi Bold 600, Bold 700
- **Style:** Warm, readable, trustworthy
- **CSS:** `font-family: 'Lora', serif;`

## Aesthetic & Background Vibes

### Solid Color Backgrounds
Most posts use flat, muted color fills — soft pinks, creams, beiges, and blues. The text does all the talking.

### Textured Backgrounds
- **Linen/Fabric texture** — Pink woven fabric feel (IMG_5953)
- **Paper grain** — Subtle paper texture on cream/beige backgrounds (IMG_5955, IMG_5967, IMG_5968)
- **Matte finish** — Smooth, flat color with zero noise

### Dark Mode
- Charcoal/near-black with white serif text
- Drop cap lettering for dramatic opening

### Design Patterns
- **Repetition style** — same phrase repeated, growing bolder (e.g., "god is with you." x9, "one day at a time." x10)
- **Centered minimal** — single phrase, tons of whitespace
- **Mixed weight** — bold + italic in same line for emphasis ("You **are** not *forgotten*.")
- **Lowercase intentional** — softness through all-lowercase ("god is with you.", "you are deeply loved.")
- **Yellow highlight** — accent highlight behind key phrase ("God already handled.")

---

## Tone of Voice
- Encouraging, never preachy
- Short, punchy statements
- God-centered but approachable
- Speaks to "her" — faith-driven women
- Lowercase = soft, uppercase = powerful
- Period at the end = certainty, finality
- Repetition = letting truth sink in
