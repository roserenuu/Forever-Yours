## How to Use This File

Just write what you want in plain English. Examples:

- "I want more lowercase text — it feels softer"
- "Stop using so much pink, I want more beige and cream lately"
- "My carousels should feel like @womenofjoy — minimal and clean"
- "I love the repetition style where the same phrase repeats and gets bolder"
- "Use more white backgrounds with dark brown text"
- "I want the vibe of my IMG_5967.jpg reference — paper texture, centered text"
- "No more decorative lines or dots — keep it ultra clean"

Iris will follow whatever you write here OVER everything else. Update it anytime.
